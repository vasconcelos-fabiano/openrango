Why OpenRango?

OpenRango is an open-source ERP designed specifically for restaurants, cafeterias, snack bars and food businesses that want complete control over their own infrastructure and data.

Features

  • Orders
  • Products
  • Pricing
  • Labels
  • Production
  • Inventory
  • Reports
  • REST API

Project philosophy

  • Open Source
  • AGPL License
  • No vendor lock-in
  • Your data belongs to you
  • Transparent development
